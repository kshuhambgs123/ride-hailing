## 3. How we use your information

- Provide, operate, and improve the Service (matching, routing, fare
  estimation, scheduling, recommendations).
- Verify driver identity and eligibility (KYC/Aadhar verification).
- Process payments and manage your in-app wallet.
- Enable real-time features: live location sharing during a trip, in-app
  chat, push notifications for ride status.
- Safety: emergency SOS, sharing live trip location with your chosen
  emergency contacts, investigating incidents.
- Trust & fraud prevention, audit logging of sensitive account actions.
- Customer support and dispute resolution.
- Marketing communications about offers/referrals, where you have opted in
  (you can opt out at any time).
- Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax, law-enforcement requests, KYC
  regulations).

## 4. Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR, where applicable)

Where the GDPR applies to you, we rely on: performance of a contract (to run
the Service you asked for), legitimate interests (safety, fraud prevention,
service improvement), consent (marketing, background location, camera/photo
access), and legal obligation (KYC/AML, tax, law enforcement).

## 5. How we share information

- **Drivers and riders**, with each other, only to the extent needed to
  complete a trip (name, photo, rating, live location during the trip, chat).
- **Payment processors** (e.g., Stripe) to process payments; we do not store
  full card numbers ourselves.
- **Cloud infrastructure providers** who host our servers, database, and file
  storage, acting strictly on our instructions.
- **KYC/identity verification providers** as required to verify Aadhar/ID
  documents.
- **Emergency contacts** you designate, if you trigger SOS or enable live
  trip sharing.
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, where required by law or to protect the
  safety of users.
- We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties for their own
  marketing purposes.
